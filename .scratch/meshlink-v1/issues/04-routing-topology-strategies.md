# Which routing and topology strategies suit phone BLE meshes?

Type: research
Status: open

## Question

Survey literature and implementations: managed flooding with dedup/TTL, gossip, Babel/distance vector, AODV/on-demand, OLSR, DTN/epidemic and spray-and-wait (for store-and-forward), and neighbor selection/topology formation under per-phone connection limits. Assess behaviour at the provisional density classes and mesh sizes, control overhead, convergence, energy. Output: cited shortlist of candidates for a simulation comparison.
