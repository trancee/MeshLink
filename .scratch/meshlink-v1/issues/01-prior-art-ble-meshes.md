# What do existing BLE P2P meshes teach us?

Type: research
Status: open

## Question

Survey bitchat, Briar, Bridgefy, Meshtastic, Berty/Wesh, Apple Multipeer Connectivity and Bluetooth SIG Mesh (incl. proxy/GATT bearer). For each: transport, discovery, routing/flooding, security, density behaviour, documented failures and published evaluations. Verify whether SIG Mesh fits phone-to-phone meshes and why/why not. Output: cited comparison and lessons for MeshLink.
