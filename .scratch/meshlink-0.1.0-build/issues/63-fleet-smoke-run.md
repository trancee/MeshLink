# Pass the 15-phone fleet smoke run

Status: ready-for-human
Type: HITL
Blocked by: 62

## Behavior

Every reachable lab phone, at least 15, runs the 0.1.0 build together and carries traffic for 10 minutes. No phone crashes or shows an ANR, and every message is delivered. The run becomes a T9 evidence record. It proves the build holds up on many real phones at once. It does not prove the density bands, which stay simulator evidence.

## Spec and ADR references

- [ADR 0017](../../../docs/adr/0017-testing-architecture.md#amendments), 2026-11-13 amendment: "Fleet smoke run" (at least 15 phones, 10 minutes, no crash, full delivery; density bands labeled as simulator evidence; no background claim) and "Pass criteria".
- [ADR 0017](../../../docs/adr/0017-testing-architecture.md#decision) decision 11: density bands come from the [routing-simulator evidence](../../../docs/evidence/2026-11-routing-simulation/README.md) and are not radio truth.
- [ADR 0005](../../../docs/adr/0005-toolchain-and-dependency-baseline.md#decision), "Device-lab signing": each free team covers at most 3 devices, so at most 6 iPhones can join.
- [Ticket 61](61-lab-faults.md): the list of at least 15 reachable phones.
- [Device-lab lessons](../../../docs/agents/lessons.md#device-lab).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An agent runs `tools/lab/` from a dedicated worktree at the same commit as ticket 62's run, or records why the commit changed and reruns ticket 62's failed scenarios.
- [ ] Every phone on ticket 61's reachable list takes part, at least 15, by alias. Missing phones are listed with the reason.
- [ ] The run lasts at least 10 minutes of traffic under `Classical`, with the traffic mix of the fleet scenario that `tools/lab/` defines, recorded in the evidence record. If `tools/lab/` has no fleet scenario, the ticket stops and an AFK ticket adds one first.
- [ ] **Pass:** every message is delivered within its lifetime, exactly once and intact, receipts match, and no phone crashes or shows an ANR during the run. A miss blocks the release unless the record root-causes it to an external fault. A product bug becomes a new ticket with a `VirtualAir` regression case.
- [ ] Per-phone delivery counts, crashes, ANRs, the diagnostic codes seen (from `diagnostics.platformLog`) and link counts are in the record.
- [ ] `python3 tools/lab/lab.py evidence --run <dir>` writes the record with every T9 field, pseudonymized Bluetooth addresses and aliases only. The record says that density bands are simulator evidence and that background behavior is unverified.
- [ ] The `CHANGELOG.md` "Unreleased" section links the record. `python3 tools/check_private.py` and the docs checks pass.
