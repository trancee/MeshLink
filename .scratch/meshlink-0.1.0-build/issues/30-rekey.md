# Rekey long-lived links

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: AFK
Blocked by: 28

## Behavior

Secured links rekey on a time and record-count schedule, so one Noise key never protects too much traffic.

- **Frame:** `REKEY` (`0x05`), 1 B, sent only on encrypted links.
- **Sender:** after sending the record that carries `REKEY`, it calls Noise `Rekey()` on its sending cipher state.
- **Receiver:** after processing that record, it calls `Rekey()` on its receiving cipher state. Each direction rekeys independently.
- **Schedule (D-21):** a sender rekeys a direction after 3,600 s or 65,536 records in that direction, whichever comes first. Time comes from the injected `Clock`.
- **Enforcement:** a receiver closes the link with `PROTOCOL_ERROR` if a direction exceeds 131,072 records without a rekey.
- **`Unsecured` receiver (owner decision, 2026-11-13):** drops a `REKEY` frame and records a diagnostic, never closing the link, the same rule as an unknown link-local frame ([link.md, Link-local frame summary](../../../docs/spec/link.md#link-local-frame-summary)).

Ticket 31 checks that rekeying still works across `BEARER_SWITCH`.

## Outcome

- **Owner decisions (2026-11-17):** Q1 each sender sends `REKEY` 3,600 s on its monotonic clock after its last rekey, whether or not it has other traffic. Q2 one key encrypts at most 65,536 records, its `REKEY` the last of them. Q3 the receiver closes with `PROTOCOL_ERROR` at the 131,073rd record under one key. Q4 `ML3007 UnknownLinkFrameDropped(type)` for a `REKEY` on an `Unsecured` link and for every unknown link-local type, recorded as D-48. Q5 the 3-hour test checks rekeys and open links; direct messages across a rekey move to ticket 46. Q6 a tier 2 `link-transport.json` from the Rust reference (snow).
- **Spec:** link.md "Rekey" states the timer, the record count, the receive limit, the handshake and old-key cases and the `Unsecured` rule (D-48). The vectors README lists `link-transport.json` as published; `check_spec.py` checks its tier 2 schema.
- **Core:** the internal `LinkCipher` in `meshlink-core` seals and opens records, inserts the count `REKEY` and reports `Overdue`. `Links` runs one timer per secured link, sends the timed `REKEY` without cancellation so no record is torn, and stops it when the link closes.
- **Test fakes:** a scripted central now keeps granting credits (`keepGranting`) when a test holds its link for hours, as link.md "Flow control" requires; without it, hourly `REKEY`s used up the window and `RotationTest` hung.
